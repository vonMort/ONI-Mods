# Compact Smart Battery

A compact, wall-mounted Smart Battery for Oxygen Not Included.
Save floor space without giving up automation control.

## Features

- **1x2 footprint:** mounts on a back wall instead of standing on the floor.
  Both occupied cells require a back wall.
- **10 kJ base capacity:** half the capacity of the vanilla Smart Battery.
- **250 kg refined metal:** slightly more expensive than the vanilla Smart Battery.
- **Smart automation:** the same configurable charge thresholds as the Smart Battery,
  with an automation output in the top cell and a power connection in the bottom cell.
- **Custom graphics:** a charge bar, a red/green automation indicator, and
  subtle vibration with flashing bolts while charging.
- **Same research:** unlocks alongside the vanilla Smart Battery.

All other stats match the vanilla Smart Battery.

## Compatibility

Supports the base game and Spaced Out! No DLC or other mods are required.
English and German building text are included; standard Smart Battery descriptions
and automation tooltips use the game's selected language.

Compatible with Bigger Capacity: its battery multiplier also applies here.
For example, a 2x multiplier increases this battery's capacity to 20 kJ.
